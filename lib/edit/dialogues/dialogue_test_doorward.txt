# Dialogue for the Dialogue Test quest.

V:1
DIALOG:dialogue_test_doorward
NPC:Doorward of the Hidden Gate
START:greeting

NODE:greeting
TEXT:The doorward studies you in silence, then lowers his spear.
TEXT:"Few come to this forgotten post. Fewer still come with questions."
OPTION:Ask who he serves.:service
OPTION:Ask about the sealed path.:sealed_path
OPTION:Leave him to his watch.:END

NODE:service
TEXT:"I serve memory first, and my hidden lord second. Both are poor masters."
EFFECT:SET_FLAG=asked_doorward_service
OPTION:Ask what he remembers.:memory:WHEN=flag.asked_doorward_service
OPTION:Return to other matters.:greeting

NODE:sealed_path
TEXT:"Beyond this place lies an errand of knives, oaths, and half-truths."
TEXT:"If you would walk it, learn first how words can open wounds."
EFFECT:SET_FLAG=heard_of_sealed_path
OPTION:Ask who sealed it.:sealed_by:WHEN=flag.heard_of_sealed_path
OPTION:Return to other matters.:greeting

NODE:memory
WHEN:flag.asked_doorward_service
TEXT:"I remember a house where every answer made another enemy."
TEXT:"That is why I test questions before I test blades."
OPTION:Return to other matters.:greeting

NODE:sealed_by
WHEN:flag.heard_of_sealed_path
TEXT:"Not Morgoth. Not wholly. Some doors are barred by those who fear rescue."
OPTION:Return to other matters.:greeting
